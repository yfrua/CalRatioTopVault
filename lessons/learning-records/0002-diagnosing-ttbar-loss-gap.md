# Physical and Information-Theoretic Causes of the ttbar Loss Discrepancy

Diagnosed the regression in Signal Region validation loss from ~0.20 (dijet) to ~0.51 (ttbar). Established that this elevation is primarily driven by the physical overlap of heavy-flavor $b$-hadron secondary vertices with displaced LLP signal signatures (raising the Bayes error rate and conditional entropy), reinforced by two-objective Pareto competition against Control Region loss. Future HPO runs should evaluate ROC AUC / background rejection rather than expecting the cross-entropy loss to reach dijet levels.
