# Root Causes of the ttbar HPO Performance Regression and Mitigation Established

Diagnosed the regression in Signal Region validation loss from 0.244 (previous ttbar HPO round) to >0.50 (current round). Established via empirical retraining that the regression is not an irreducible Bayes error rate floor of the bugfixed ttbar dataset, but an optimization failure driven by: (1) discrepancy between trial selection (normalized distance) and checkpoint callbacks (raw square root Euclidean distance), (2) cohort-dependent pruning distortions on early warmup schedules, (3) MOTPE Pareto drift toward single-objective Control Region outliers, and (4) search space dilution from dynamic epoch budgets. Replaced normalized distance with raw Euclidean distance $\sqrt{\mathcal{L}_{\text{SR}}^2 + \mathcal{L}_{\text{CR}}^2}$ in `hp_opt/objective.py` to match `ComplementPerformanceWriter`, eliminating cohort extremes dependence and restoring 1:1 fidelity with model checkpointing.
